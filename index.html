About me

Thanks for stopping by! My name is Wilhelm. As a digital product designer, I want to create software that’s fair to all the people who use it. Products should be fun and empower people to achieve their goals in a safe environment. As such I strive for ethical, inclusive and privacy-focused products with a clear vision.

Outside of work, I am a dancer who specializes in breaking and house dance. I also enjoy photography, coding, and speaking different languages.
